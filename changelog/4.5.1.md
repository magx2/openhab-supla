## 4.5.1

Unreleased version 4.5.1 - native device sleep and ping recovery

### Fixed

- Fixed false offline transitions and socket timeout, reconnect, and cleanup warnings for sleep-capable native devices such as ZAMEL THW-01.
- Preserved reported sensor value validity across sleep intervals and activity-timeout negotiation so sleeping devices retain their cached readings until expiry.
- Fixed repeated `Ping is not running` warnings by restarting monitoring when sleeping or ordinary devices resume sending data after ping expiry.
